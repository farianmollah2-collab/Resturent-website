Our Story

A Culinary Journey
Since 2018

Savoria was born from a simple belief: that food brings people together in the most meaningful way. Founded by Chef Alessandro Romano, our restaurant blends traditional cooking techniques with modern culinary innovation.

Every dish tells a story, every ingredient is carefully selected, and every meal is crafted with passion. We source locally, cook seasonally, and serve with genuine hospitality.
